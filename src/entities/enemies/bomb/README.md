# Bomb

Enemies that kamikaze when near the player and must be killed before reaching
them. Add them here as `bomb/<enemy_name>/`.

- **Bomber Skeleton** (`bomber_skeleton/`) — a fast skeleton (faster than the
  player) that chases the player and, when close, arms a 2s fuse and detonates
  for heavy area damage. No contact damage. Spawns one at a time, min difficulty 3.

# Patch 14.3.3

## New Relics

The relic pool is now full at **30** (22 normal + 8 cursed). Four new relics join the roster:

- **Smith's Hammer** — Anvils have a 10% chance to be usable again.
- **Badge of Mastery** — Weapons can take a second signature upgrade.
- **Zephyr's Pinion** — Evasion also increases your movement speed.
- **CHa0s** *(cursed)* — You can no longer choose upgrades from level-ups, anvils, or Winged Boots — they are chosen at random, but their effects are tripled. Relic and ascension choices stay yours.

## Relic Reworks

- **Pointy Tips** *(new)* — Thorns can now critically strike.
- **Vampiric Rage** — Reworked: now grants 5% lifesteal on every hit instead of healing off crits.
- **Burning Ire** — Non-crit damage reduced by 95% (crits are everything now).
- **Unstable Mind** — Clarified wording (lucky re-roll of the chosen upgrade).
- **Corrosive Burst** — The burst now also triggers when your poison damage-over-time kills an enemy.
- **Soul Harvest** — Souls only drop from enemies already decaying.
- **Static Conduit** — Shock now chains to 2 nearby enemies instead of 1.
- **Avarice** — Reworked: +0.001% damage per gold held (hoard to hit harder), but you lose 10% of your gold when you take damage — capped at once per second so multi-hits don't drain everything.

## Quality of Life

- Player health bar moved to the **top** of the character (above the head) instead of the bottom.
- Crit damage from level-ups buffed (0.15 → 0.20 per pick) so it no longer feels equal to Power.

## Compendium

- Ailment and element names are now **color-coded** to match their element across relic descriptions, the relic choice menu, and the compendium (burn = orange, shock = yellow, poison = green, slow = blue, crit = purple, decay = olive, brand = tan, impale = gray) — so you can see at a glance which element an effect belongs to.
- The compendium now lists the **Rogue, Berserker, and Engineer** loadouts that were previously missing: Dual Stab, Smoke Bomb, Spin Axe, Axe Throw, Grenade Launcher, and Deploy Turret.

## Fixes & Polish

- Fixed several script warnings for smoother, cleaner runs (book weapon, poison spray/puff, anvil menu, multiplayer controller).
